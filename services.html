How Your 4-Step Recovery Journey Works

From Day 1 root-cause biomechanical scanning to permanent athletic resilience, our structured 4-phase pathway leaves zero room for recurring injuries.

01
Day 1 Intake

Step 1: Friendly Movement Check

We do not just press where it hurts. We run high-speed motion capture, surface EMG, and joint load analysis to isolate the exact biomechanical origin of your pain.

Personalized Kinematic Report
02
Weeks 1 – 2

Step 2: Relieve the Pain

Immediate symptom suppression utilizing radial acoustic shockwave, computer spinal decompression, and targeted manual release to turn off chronic nerve guard loops.

Target: 70%+ Pain Drop
03
Weeks 3 – 5

Step 3: Rebuild Easy Movement

Restoring inhibited stabilizers via unweighted AlterG locomotion, eccentric muscle loading, and dynamic proprioceptive balance plates to correct muscle firing order.

Symmetrical Joint Stability

Tissue tolerance must exceed daily life stress demands. We test power production on dual force plates and transition your exercise regime into our digital home portal.
