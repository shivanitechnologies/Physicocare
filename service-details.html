What Is Orthopedic Physiotherapy?

Orthopedic physiotherapy helps manage conditions affecting muscles, joints, bones and soft tissues. Treatment focuses on restoring movement, improving strength and supporting everyday activities.

✓

Better Mobility

Restore joint range and smooth, fluid movement.

✓

Pain Management

Target root mechanical causes of discomfort.

✓

Improved Strength

Build joint stability and everyday endurance.

How Your Treatment Progresses
