What is This Treatment?

Orthopedic physiotherapy focuses on restoring structural alignment and painless mobility to bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, and surrounding myofascial tissues. Using manual joint mobilization combined with targeted neurological muscle retraining, we accelerate your body's natural regenerative timeline in private suites.
