Our 4-Step Recovery Path

1

Step 1: Gentle Spinal Relief

Gentle spinal stretches and press-up exercises take pressure off your nerves and soothe pinched disc pain.

2

Step 2: Soothing Joint Movement

Gentle hands-on therapy loosens stiff joints in your back and neck, making it easy to turn, bend, and move freely.

3

Step 3: Core & Back Strengthening

Easy core exercises teach your deep abdominal and back muscles to support your spine like a natural belt.

4

Step 4: Everyday Posture & Staying Strong

Practical everyday habits, safe lifting techniques, and simple desk posture tips make sure your back stays pain-free for life.
