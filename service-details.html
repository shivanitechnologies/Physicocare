The 4-Phase Spine Recovery Pathway

1

Mechanical Decompression & Centralization

Utilizing computerized passive axial decompression and directional preference exercises to draw disc nucleus pulposus away from spinal nerve roots.

2

Joint Mobilization & Segmental Freedom

Gentle Grade III-IV manual thoracic and lumbar mobilizations to eliminate hypomobile compensation in adjacent vertebral segments.

3

Deep Core & Multifidus Muscular Bracing

Ultrasound biofeedback activation of the transversus abdominis and spinal rotatores, creating an internal hydraulic cylinder that shields your discs.

4

Dynamic Loading & Ergonomic Hardening

Hip-hinge retraining, deadlift patterning, and personalized workstation setup ensuring 0% pain recurrence during work and sports.
