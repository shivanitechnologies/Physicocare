FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Do I need a doctor's referral to schedule an appointment? ↓

No. PhysioCare operates under direct clinical access. You can book an appointment directly with our doctors of physical therapy without needing a prior physician prescription.

What should I wear to my initial physical evaluation? ↓

Wear comfortable athletic clothing (shorts, t-shirt, or activewear) that permits unrestricted movement of the affected joint, spine, or limb.

How soon can I expect to notice pain reduction? ↓

Most patients notice meaningful mobility improvement and decreased muscular guarding during or immediately following their first manual therapy session.
