The Science of Healing

How Physical Therapy Actually Fixes What Hurts

Physiotherapy is the proven medical science of restoring natural joint alignment, relieving compressed nerves, and retraining movement patterns—without reliance on medications or invasive surgery.

1. Hands-On Manual Therapy

Your therapist uses clinical hands-on joint mobilization, myofascial trigger release, and passive spinal traction to unlock restricted joints, decrease inflammation, and provide immediate pain relief.

Immediate Relief Focus

2. Corrective Exercise & Gait

Biomechanical Restoration

Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know Before Your Visit

Quick, honest answers so you can book your first consultation with total peace of mind.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a physical therapist?

No! Under Direct Access laws, you can schedule an evaluation with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy directly without needing to see an MD first or obtain a prescription.

Does health insurance cover my physical therapy treatments?

Yes, physical therapy is an essential medical benefit. We are in-network with Medicare, BlueCross, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare, and we also accept HSA/FSA cards. We verify your benefits for free before your visit.

How many sessions will it take for me to feel better?

Most patients feel noticeable relief within their first 2 to 3 sessions. After your initial 60-minute evaluation, your doctor will give you an exact roadmap with estimated weeks to 100% recovery.

What should I wear to my first appointment?

Wear comfortable, athletic clothing that allows your therapist to easily examine the joint or area that hurts (e.g. gym shorts for knee/hip pain, a tank top or loose t-shirt for shoulder/neck pain).
