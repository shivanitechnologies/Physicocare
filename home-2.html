Our Clinical Protocol

The 4-Stage Recovery Roadmap

Eliminating chronic inflammation is only phase one. We systematically rebuild tissue resilience and neuromuscular memory for lifelong durability.

01

Diagnostic Isolation

Pinpoint the mechanical insult through dynamic ultrasound, passive joint mobility testing, and spine palpation.

Goal: Identify primary generator
02

Pain De-escalation

Extracorporeal shockwave therapy, myofascial release, and joint traction to interrupt chronic pain signaling loops.

Goal: 80%+ acute symptom reduction
03

Neuromotor Retraining

Reprogram firing sequences in inhibited muscle groups (gluteus medius, transversus abdominis, rotator cuff).

Goal: Symmetrical joint stability
04

Load Capacity & Guarding

Progressive eccentric overload and sport-specific plyometrics ensuring tissue tolerance exceeds future stress demands.

Goal: Permanent zero-recurrence
