FAQ

Questions Patients Ask

Clear, transparent answers regarding doctor referrals, diagnostic exams, insurance coverage, and our 1-on-1 rehabilitation model.

No referral is required. Under direct access legislation, you can be directly assessed and treated by our Board-Certified Doctors of Physical Therapy immediately. If your condition warrants MRI imaging or orthopedic surgery, we coordinate directly with your primary physician.
Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting athletic clothing that allows observation of joint mechanics and spine motion. Your initial session includes an uninterrupted 60-minute biomechanical exam, objective range-of-motion testing, and hands-on doctoral treatment on day one.
Yes, we work with major PPO insurance networks, Medicare, and HSA/FSA plans. Our clinical care coordinators verify your deductible, copay, and out-of-pocket maximum free of charge before your visit, ensuring complete financial transparency with zero surprise facility fees.
Recovery timelines depend on tissue healing stages and your personalized rehabilitation goals. Most patients with acute sprains, posture strains, or nerve impingement feel substantial improvement within 4 to 6 sessions. Complex post-surgical cases typically follow an 8 to 12-week progression.
We never double-book patients or pass your treatment to untrained aides. Every minute of your appointment is dedicated exclusively to you with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy practicing advanced manual joint mobilization, dry needling, and targeted neuromuscular re-education.
Yes! We provide secure, HIPAA-compliant virtual physical therapy consultations. Through interactive video, our clinicians analyze your biomechanics, evaluate your workstation ergonomic setup, and guide you through progressive therapeutic exercises in real time.
