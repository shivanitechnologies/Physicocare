QUESTIONS & CLARITY

Pricing & Booking FAQs

Do I need a doctor's referral or prescription to book? ▼

In California, Direct Access laws allow you to be evaluated and treated by a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy directly without a physician referral for up to 45 days or 12 visits. If prolonged care is needed, our intake team coordinates directly with your primary physician.

What happens during my first Basic Assessment? ▼

Your physical therapist performs an in-depth orthopaedic and biomechanical examination, evaluates joint range of motion, tests muscle activation, and clarifies your recovery timeline. You also receive an initial exercise protocol to begin relief immediately.

Can I switch from Standard to Comprehensive Premium care? ▼

Yes, your care plan is completely flexible. If your recovery progresses faster than expected or if you require advanced biomechanical gait capture, your clinician can adjust session lengths accordingly with no penalty.
